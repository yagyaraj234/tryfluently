# End-of-Lesson Quiz, Practice Rounds and Follow-up Lessons

Updated 25 October 2026. Partly supersedes [0003](0003-paged-lessons-generated-on-start.md): one question per Page shown under the reading, and "finishing is instant" right after the last answer. Generation on Start, the planner and parallel Page-writers, typed Blocks, instant grading and late-feedback score reconciliation still stand.

## Decisions

**Pages are formatted.** A Page body is short paragraphs and bullet lists, with the example set apart. Each Page has 2–4 **Key Terms** shown in bold and 0–3 **Glossary Terms**, words the Learner may not know, shown with a dotted underline that opens a short definition when tapped or focused. The model still writes plain text: Key Terms and Glossary Terms are separate fields that must appear word for word in the body, and a Page that breaks this is rejected and written again like any other.

**Pages only teach.** Page-writers get the idea, never a question Block, so a Page does not restate an exercise. A vocab card stays on a Page as a teaching card when the plan asks for one; it is not quizzed.

**The planner sets the questions.** It gives each Page 1–3 question Blocks, more where the idea is dense or the Learner is weak on that skill. A Lesson has at most 10. A plan that breaks either rule is rejected.

**The Quiz comes at the end.** After the last Page the Learner answers the **Quiz** one question at a time, with no feedback in between, then sees every result together: question, answer, verdict, correct answer and explanation. Rewrite and speaking answers show "checking" until their feedback arrives. The question agent writes the Quiz from the finished Pages. Questions live in their own list, independent of Pages, so later rounds can add to it.

**The Learner chooses what's next.** Under the results a choice offers **Practice more**, **Learn more** and **Next lesson**. The Lesson completes only on Next lesson or Learn more: that is when it is marked complete, the Unit advances, scores and XP are applied and the analysis is queued. A Learner who leaves at the choice comes back to the results and choice.

**Practice Rounds.** Practice more writes 5 new questions in one model call from the Lesson's Pages and the questions the Learner got wrong, leaning toward those mistakes. They are checked like Quiz questions and never repeat an earlier one. Each round has its own results, then the choice again. Rounds repeat as often as the Learner likes, and their answers count in that Lesson's scores and XP.

**Follow-up Lessons.** Learn more takes an optional note on what confused the Learner, completes the current Lesson and inserts a **Follow-up Lesson** right after it in the Unit, moving later Lessons down one place. It is generated at once and the Learner is taken into it. It re-teaches the same objective from a different angle, aimed at the wrong answers and the note. It is a normal, required Lesson: it gates the next one and the Unit is not mastered until it is complete. A chain has at most 2 Follow-up Lessons after an original Lesson; after that the choice offers only Practice more and Next lesson.

## Existing data

Lessons started before this change keep their content version, format and player: questions on Pages and Finish after the last answer. New Lessons carry a new content version.
